E-102 HEAT EXCHANGER - MATERIAL AND CHANNEL-HEAD FIXES

Changes in this build:
1. Equipment generation now initializes the E-102 material library when no
   material dictionary is supplied.
2. Shell and both channel heads use the transparent blue inspection material.
   Tubes, tubesheets, baffles, flanges, bolts and supports retain metallic/
   solid materials.
3. Blender 5.x transparency uses DITHERED surface rendering, with a guarded
   BLENDED fallback for older builds.
4. Left channel head is explicitly generated toward -X; right head toward +X.
   No negative object scale or manual mirroring is required.
5. Channel-head base references are positioned at the outer faces of the
   tubesheets, with the configured 10 mm lap applied by geometry.py.
6. Channel-head axial depth now uses channel_head_length_m. The generated
   geometry is an ellipsoidal hollow head rather than a hemisphere whose depth
   was incorrectly fixed to the shell radius.
7. Tube-side nozzles and flanges are positioned from the channel-head dome apex.
8. Tubesheet radius now includes shell wall thickness and edge allowance.

Validation performed:
- Python compileall passed.
- ZIP integrity passed.
- Not yet executed inside Blender 5.3; please rebuild and inspect before
  treating the geometry as runtime-verified.
