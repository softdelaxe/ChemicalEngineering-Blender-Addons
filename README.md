# Chemical Engineering Blender Add-ons

**Engineering design, process simulation and 3D visualization tools developed for Blender.**

This repository brings together Python-based tools for chemical engineering, process engineering, equipment design and engineering visualization.

The objective is to connect engineering principles, mathematical calculations and computational modelling with interactive 3D representations.

## Engineering Approach

Our development approach follows a practical engineering workflow:

1. Understand the physical process.
2. Establish the design basis and assumptions.
3. Perform engineering calculations.
4. Implement computational models using Python.
5. Generate and visualize engineering systems in Blender.
6. Evaluate results and validate the design basis.

## Available Add-ons

### 1. E-102 Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchanger Designer

**Version:** 1.1.2  
**Blender compatibility:** 5.2+

A Python-based Blender extension for preliminary shell-and-tube heat exchanger design and 3D visualization.

Project capabilities include:

- Process design inputs and fluid properties.
- Thermal design calculations.
- Equipment geometry generation.
- Heat exchanger assembly visualization.
- Materials and equipment representation.
- Flow visualization.

**Project directory:** `Addons/E102_Heat_Exchanger/`

**Development status:** Under development.
